## About us

Myntlo is meeting intelligence for small teams.
Upload a recording or record right in your browser, and Myntlo writes up the summary, the decisions, the action items with owners, and the open questions.

Most tools summarize the meeting you just had.
Myntlo reads across every meeting your team has ever had, and tells you when a decision got quietly reversed, when an action item has stalled for weeks, or when the same blocker keeps coming back.

## What you get

- **Decision log** - every decision in one searchable place, with shareable links
- **Action items** - owners, status, and a heads-up when something stalls
- **Pre-meeting briefs** - open items and recent decisions before you walk in
- **Ask your meeting** - chat with the full transcript
- **No bot on the call** - nobody wants a fourth participant named "Notetaker"
